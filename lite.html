New to Accord? Start here Three simple steps
01 / AGREE

Write down what done means.

The client names the work, adds the freelancer’s public wallet address and sets clear delivery conditions. Start with an editable example in the agreement form.

02 / SHARE

Bring the other person in.

Choose “Export & share agreement” and send that file privately. The freelancer connects their own wallet, chooses “Open a shared agreement” and selects the file.

03 / FINISH

Follow the next step.

The client funds the milestone. The freelancer submits delivery. The client checks the work and approves release. Accord shows the action available to your wallet.

Moving from desktop to phone?

Your wallet and your agreement file are separate. Restore your wallet privately in your wallet app, then export the agreement from Accord on desktop and open that file in Accord on your phone. Connect the same public address on Testnet. Never enter a recovery phrase or secret key into Accord.

A transaction is taking longer than expected?

Use Refresh to check the original transaction. Keep the browser’s saved agreement and wait for a definite result before trying again. Refreshing the page does not cancel an onchain transaction.
