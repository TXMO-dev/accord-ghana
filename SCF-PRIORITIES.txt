ACCORD — ALL-PRIORITY DELIVERY PLAN
Updated 2026-11-11

Positioning: A Ghana-first collaboration neighbourhood where freelancers and
clients meet, agree on work, and manage verifiable Stellar milestones.
The 3D world supports these tasks; success is measured by completed work.

1. DISCOVERY TO AGREEMENT — first implementation slice
Deployed: signed Hiring/Available public opportunity posts in the
Lounge. Available posts can prefill a reviewed client draft with the posting
wallet and editable scope. Hiring posts let interested people draft a public
introduction. Existing 24-hour visibility and moderation apply.
Not implemented: private invitations, acceptance state, saved/closed opportunity
board, full opportunity search. Public interest is not contract acceptance.
Next: participant-authorized invite/decline/accept records, wallet-bound replay
protection, expiry, private reads, idempotent acceptance, agreement linkage.
Acceptance: two wallets meet, accept an invitation and create a milestone without
copying an address; unrelated wallets cannot read invitations or accept them.

2. LINKED PROJECT MILESTONES
Implemented: project IDs and named, ordered stage grouping preserved across
wallet-scoped drafts, editable plans and agreement export/import. Saved stages
link to each other; duplicate saved stages are blocked. Plans can be reset and
drafts detached. Each stage still creates an independent escrow agreement.
Next: participant-owned project room and cross-device duplicate prevention;
show budget, funded/released totals and next action using verified chain state.
Ordering is planning metadata unless explicitly enforced by a reviewed contract.
Acceptance: reload/import restores project grouping; retries do not duplicate
stages; actual funds never inferred from chat events or local receipts alone.

3. SCOPE CHANGE CONTROL
Existing: acceptance-condition hashes, delivery checklist and revision requests.
Next: immutable proposal containing original terms hash, new scope, reason and
price/time effect; explicit approvals from each party and version history.
Current funded contract terms must not be silently overwritten. Changed financial
terms require a separately reviewed contract transition or replacement agreement.
Acceptance: stale proposals cannot replace newer terms; one party cannot approve
for the other; old and proposed obligations remain visibly distinct.

4. OPT-IN PROOF OF COMPLETED WORK
Existing: transaction receipts and opt-in Lounge introductions/skills.
Next: voluntary work cards linking contract, network, agreement and release receipt;
minimal public metadata; no confidential briefs/delivery URLs published by default.
Verify release on chain before awarding completed status. A wallet signature does
not establish legal identity or quality. Do not equate dispute settlement with
successful delivery. Self-dealing/duplicate receipts must not inflate reputation.
Acceptance: another browser verifies the receipt; revoked consent removes public
profile display; testnet history is clearly separated from real-money activity.

5. DISPUTE NEGOTIATION
Existing: freeze/dispute and two-party shared-room settlement approvals.
Next: structured reason, proposed split, private evidence references, counteroffer
history and expiry/re-sign guidance. Show exact refund and freelancer amount.
Mediation is a separate future contract design: authority and mediator must be
agreed before funding. No unilateral administrator withdrawal in current flow.
Acceptance: expired/wrong-network/wrong-agreement approvals rejected; both wallets
approve exactly the same split; no payment occurs solely from a room comment.

6. PAYMENT USABILITY AND GHANA PAYOUT VALIDATION
Existing: working Testnet escrow and Mainnet preparation helpers.
Next: asset-aware amounts/decimals, fees/reserve/trustline preflight, stablecoin
selection only for explicitly deployed and verified contract/asset pairs, recovery
from signing cancellation and network mismatch. Mainnet still needs funded deploy,
initialization, configuration verification and end-to-end live validation.
Ghana payout path requires an identified provider, Ghana coverage, compliance,
pricing and settlement confirmation. Paystack's Sikani correspondence does NOT
establish Stellar stablecoin-to-mobile-money support or an Accord partnership.
Acceptance: verified asset/network/issuer, displayed fees, confirmed receipts,
partner sandbox payout reconciliation before any claim of cash-out support.

7. LOW-BANDWIDTH AND ACCESSIBLE WORKSPACE
Existing: focus mode and conventional agreement controls inside the 3D experience.
Next: explicit lightweight entry that does not initialize Three.js; equivalent
agreement, room and recovery actions; keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels.
Acceptance: no world assets downloaded in lightweight mode; mobile keyboard does
not obscure submission; slow connection and WebGL failure preserve core tasks.

DELIVERY ORDER
A: opportunity handoff (source implementation in this change), then invites and
linked projects. B: scope proposals and verifiable work cards. C: dispute history
and lightweight parity. D: asset/payment readiness and separately validated payout
integration. Track all seven; do not enable money-related changes just to ship.

SCF EVIDENCE AND ELIGIBILITY
Round 46 deadline listed as November 9, 2026; do not promise entry after deadline.
Confirm application route with SCF and plan for the next eligible cycle if needed.
Integration track expects existing traction and eligible ecosystem building blocks.
Compare maintaining custom escrow against integrating an existing provider such
as Trustless Work; record requirements, limitations and maintenance costs honestly.

Evidence: user-reported two-wallet Testnet settlement, delivery/release, realtime
notifications and voice playback. Keep public transaction links for verification.
Automated tests are engineering evidence, not customer traction or external audit.
Still needed: Ghana freelancer/client interviews, consented pilot participants,
observed completion rates, onboarding time, failure/retry rates, paid usage if any,
security review evidence and realistic milestone budget. Do not invent metrics.

RELEASE GATES
New discovery work must pass automated tests, production build and a two-wallet
browser check before calling it live. Preserve unrelated pending visual changes.
New DB features need versioned migrations, least-privilege access, isolation tests,
rollback/recovery instructions and deployed verification. No new migration is
required for this first opportunity-post slice.
