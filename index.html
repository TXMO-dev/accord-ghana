THE PROBLEM TO VALIDATE
Payment uncertainty.
Ghanaian freelancers and small agencies working across borders need clear acceptance terms and predictable settlement. Our research will test how often delayed payment and disputes occur, their cost, and willingness to use funded milestones.
WHY STELLAR
Agreements with rules.
Soroban holds a milestone's test assets and enforces authenticated state transitions. Stellar records settlement; Varial keeps signing in the user's wallet. The contract cannot judge work quality, and local payout remains a separate provider integration.
WHY THIS FOUNDER
Built from experience.
Timothy's portfolio describes eight years of software engineering, Sikani Pay payment infrastructure, FleetCommando production systems, and Ethereum escrow experiments. Accord is a new solo project; those products' customers are not Accord customers.
