API/session/storage boundaries, production npm dependencies. Internal source review;
not an independent audit or a guarantee of Mainnet safety.

Verified in this run:

- npm audit --omit=dev reports zero known advisories. This does not rule out unknown flaws.

- Signed settlement proofs are validated against agreement state, asset, exact split,
  expiry and signature before sharing and again before transaction submission.

  mixed-network deployment from silently using Testnet RPC with a Mainnet contract ID.
