PRODUCT
Accord helps Ghanaian freelancers and international clients record acceptance conditions, fund a milestone, submit a delivery reference and approve release. The core hypothesis is that clear terms and funded milestones reduce uncertainty in direct client relationships. This has not yet been validated through interviews or real customer usage.

ARCHITECTURE
Browser workspace -> Varial signature -> Stellar RPC -> Rust/Soroban escrow contract.
The client creates and funds; the freelancer submits; the client releases. Either party may dispute funded/submitted work. Disputes freeze the milestone; both parties must authorize an exact settlement split. No arbitrator or unilateral timeout refund exists. The contract cannot judge work quality.
Terms and delivery references stay in browser-local storage or privately exported files. Hashes are onchain; addresses, amounts and state are public. There is no hosted document backup, key custody, fiat payout or mobile-money integration.

DIFFERENTIATION AND REUSE DECISION
Trustless Work already provides milestone escrow infrastructure on Stellar. Accord does not claim to invent escrow. Proposed differentiation: Ghana-focused onboarding, explicit acceptance terms, private document exchange and a comprehensible two-party workspace. This is not yet demonstrated customer advantage.
Before expanding the contract, compare existing escrow infrastructure against Accord's two-party consent, privacy, operational cost and recovery requirements. Record a build-versus-integrate decision and discuss track fit with SCF. No integration partnership is claimed.
