PRODUCT AND PROBLEM
Accord is a milestone escrow workspace for Ghanaian software/design freelancers and small agencies working with international clients. They agree acceptance conditions, fund a milestone, submit a delivery reference and release payment after approval. Delayed payments and unclear acceptance criteria are hypotheses to validate, not measured findings.

STELLAR ARCHITECTURE
The browser uses the Stellar SDK and Varial signing API. A Rust/Soroban contract enforces client/freelancer roles and holds native test XLM. Acceptance terms and delivery references are hashed; plaintext stays in browser-local records and privately shared files. No private backend, hosted key custody or mobile-money payout exists. Testnet contract: CASWGE2BE6QLPR7L4BDKH5RH6ITAIWKBQ2MSLYE4GNP4HDTXMU2CTCIV. Recorded WASM SHA256: 255f22766fcfb1c9c3f02478d4a5c25d9e6250217f75fce491a66ee78c10f58e. Contract SDK: 23.5.3.

COMPLETED ENGINEERING
Create, fund, submit, delivery-hash verification, release, dispute freeze, agreement-file exchange, summaries, search and transaction reviews are implemented. The new disputed-milestone settlement flow has the freelancer sign an exact split authorization, export it privately, and the client validate, review, sign and submit. Approval binds the contract, agreement ID, amount, Testnet network and ledger expiry. The client pays transaction fees. No private keys are exchanged. Approvals last 120 ledgers; deletion is not revocation. Supported freelancer signing uses a standard single-key G-address wallet; multisig/custom accounts are not claimed.
