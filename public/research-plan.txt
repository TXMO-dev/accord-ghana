ACCORD — CUSTOMER DISCOVERY GUIDE
Recruit 10 Ghana-based independent software/design professionals with recent overseas client work and, separately, 3-5 paying clients. Ask permission to take anonymized notes. Do not collect client payment details or confidential contracts.

1. Describe your most recent overseas project from agreement to final payment.
2. Which payment method did you use and why?
3. What was the quoted price, total cost and actual time to receive payment? Use ranges if preferred.
4. Tell me about the last delayed or disputed payment. What happened and how was it resolved?
5. How do you agree on acceptance conditions?
6. Have you used funded milestones or escrow? Why did you keep or stop using it?
7. What would make your client refuse this workflow?
8. How important is cedi/mobile-money payout compared with keeping digital assets?
9. Try the demo without coaching. Where do you hesitate?
10. What would need to be true for you to use it on one real project?

Record: participant pseudonym, segment, date, concrete example, existing alternative, costs, objection, observed task failures, consent for follow-up. Distinguish quotes from interpretation.
Do not lead with blockchain or ask only whether people 'like the idea'.

Go/no-go proposal: proceed to a funded pilot only if users describe a recurring problem, clients accept the workflow, at least three parties commit to a defined pilot, payout feasibility is confirmed, and security/compliance gates are satisfied. These thresholds are planning assumptions.
