01 / The product

A clear agreement before work begins.

The problem to validate

Freelancers and clients need shared acceptance terms and a clear path from funded work to approval. Interviews will test payment delays, disagreement costs and willingness to adopt this workflow. These are hypotheses, not measured customer findings.

Why Stellar is central

A Soroban contract holds test assets and checks each party's authorization. Client approval releases funds; a dispute freezes them until both parties sign a settlement. A transaction record makes the result independently inspectable.

04 / Five-minute demo

Follow both sides of the agreement.

  1. Use two separate, funded Testnet wallets. Connect as client and create a one-test-XLM agreement with the other wallet as freelancer.
  2. Review the terms and sign creation. Fund the milestone in a separate reviewed transaction.
  3. Export the agreement; reconnect as freelancer and open the file if using another browser. Submit a delivery reference.
  4. Reconnect as client, verify the reference and approve release. Inspect the transaction receipt.
  5. For a separate dispute test, raise a dispute after funding. The freelancer authorizes an exact split; the client imports that approval and signs settlement before expiry.

Raise dispute freezes the milestone; it is not the next step for normal delivery. The contract cannot judge work quality and funds can remain locked if the parties cannot agree.
